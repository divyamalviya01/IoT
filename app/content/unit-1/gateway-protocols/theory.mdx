An IoT gateway connects local devices that speak many different protocols to the internet, translating their messages into a common protocol and filtering, combining and securing the data on the way.

## Why gateways are needed

Devices in a building or factory speak many languages. Zigbee bulbs, BLE wristbands, Modbus energy meters and BACnet air-conditioners all use different protocols, and most of them cannot reach the internet on their own.

A <Term id="gateway">gateway</Term> sits between these devices and the cloud. It understands the local protocols on one side and speaks an internet protocol, usually MQTT or HTTP, on the other.

## Protocols a gateway meets

Gateways speak field protocols to machines and buildings, and messaging protocols to the cloud.

<CompareTable
  caption="Field and gateway protocols"
  columns={["Protocol", "Where it is used", "Key idea"]}
  rows={[
    ["Modbus", "Factories, energy meters, solar inverters", "Simple master–slave reads and writes of numbered registers (1979); RTU over RS-485 or TCP on port 502"],
    ["BACnet", "Building automation: air-conditioning, lighting, lifts", "Standard objects such as “analog input” for building equipment (ASHRAE 135)"],
    ["OPC UA", "Industry 4.0, connecting PLCs and factory software", "Secure, platform-independent data models for machines (IEC 62541)"],
    ["LwM2M", "Managing IoT devices remotely", "Device management over CoAP with standard objects, such as 3303 for temperature"],
    ["AMQP", "Business systems and cloud back ends", "Message queues and routing through a broker; reliable delivery"],
    ["DDS", "Robots, vehicles, defence and medical systems", "Real-time publish–subscribe without a central broker"],
    ["MQTT", "Most links from gateways to the cloud", "Lightweight publish–subscribe through a broker"],
  ]}
/>

- <Term id="modbus">Modbus</Term> is one of the oldest and most common industrial protocols. A gateway asks a meter "read registers 1 and 2" and gets back raw numbers.
- <Term id="lwm2m">LwM2M</Term> standardises device management: reading settings, rebooting and updating firmware over CoAP.
- <Term id="amqp">AMQP</Term> suits business back ends that need queues, routing rules and guaranteed delivery.
- <Term id="dds">DDS</Term> lets devices publish and subscribe directly, with no broker, and fine control over timing. ROS 2, used in robots, runs on DDS.

## How translation works

Take a Zigbee temperature sensor. It reports a raw 16-bit number in hundredths of a degree, such as 2850. The gateway:

1. **Receives** the Zigbee frame.
2. **Decodes** it and finds the value 2850 in the temperature cluster.
3. **Converts** it: 2850 × 0.01 = 28.50 °C.
4. **Publishes** it as MQTT to a topic such as `site/kitchen/temperature` with the payload `{"value": 28.5, "unit": "C"}`.

The cloud never needs to know about Zigbee. Replace the sensor with a BLE or Modbus one and the cloud sees the same message. You can try this in the **Visualize** tab and in lab L10.

<Callout kind="example" title="Smart building in a college">
A gateway in the electrical room reads 40 Modbus energy meters every second. It averages each meter over one minute and publishes 40 MQTT messages a minute instead of 2,400, so the energy dashboard stays live but the internet link and cloud bill stay small.
</Callout>

## Gateway architecture

A typical gateway has radios and ports for local devices (Zigbee, BLE, RS-485), a processor running Linux, local storage, and a secure internet link (Ethernet, Wi-Fi or 4G). Its software has protocol adapters for each device type, a rules engine, a local buffer and a cloud connector.

## Advantages and limitations of gateways

**Advantages**

- Lets old and new devices with different protocols join one system.
- Cuts internet traffic by filtering and aggregating data.
- Keeps working locally when the internet is down.
- Adds security for devices too small to protect themselves.

**Limitations**

- A single point of failure: if the gateway stops, every device behind it is cut off.
- Translation can lose detail or add delay.
- One more device to secure, update and maintain.
