## Smart home

A smart home connects lights, plugs, door locks, cameras and appliances. A Wi-Fi router or a smart-home hub acts as the gateway. Some hubs translate between Zigbee devices and Wi-Fi.

You control everything from a phone app or by voice. Rules automate routines, such as switching off all lights when the door locks at night.

## Smart grid

A <Term id="smart-grid">smart grid</Term> adds sensors and two-way communication to the electricity network. Smart meters measure each home's use every few minutes.

The meters send readings to a data concentrator, which forwards them to the electricity company. The company can read meters remotely, spot faults and outages quickly, and offer prepaid recharge through an app.

<Callout kind="example" title="Smart meters in India">
Indian electricity companies are replacing old meters with smart prepaid meters under national programmes. Consumers recharge like a mobile phone, and the company no longer needs a person to visit each home to read the meter.
</Callout>

## Connected cars

A connected car has a telematics control unit with a mobile-network modem. This is called <Term id="telematics">telematics</Term>. It sends location, speed and engine health to the carmaker's cloud.

Services include live navigation, automatic crash alerts, remote locking and software updates over the air. In India, the AIS-140 standard requires tracking devices and emergency buttons in public transport vehicles.

## Wearables

<Term id="wearable">Wearables</Term> are devices worn on the body, such as fitness bands and smartwatches. They measure steps, heart rate and sleep.

A wearable usually has no internet connection of its own. It sends data by Bluetooth Low Energy to the user's phone, which acts as the gateway.

## Supply chain and logistics

Goods are tagged with RFID labels, GPS trackers and temperature loggers. Readers in warehouses and trucks record every movement.

Companies can see where every shipment is and whether it was kept at the right temperature. India's eVIN network uses this idea to track vaccine stocks and cold-storage temperatures across the country.

## Advantages and limitations of large IoT infrastructures

**Advantages**

- One reference structure fits many industries, so designs and skills can be reused.
- Remote monitoring replaces manual visits, such as meter reading.
- Data from many devices reveals patterns that single devices cannot show.
- Services improve over time through software updates.

**Limitations**

- Many stages means many places where things can fail or be attacked.
- Building and running the infrastructure is expensive.
- Devices from different suppliers may not work together.
- Services stop if the network or the cloud goes down.
