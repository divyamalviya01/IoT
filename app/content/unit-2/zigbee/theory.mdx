## What Zigbee is

<Term id="zigbee">Zigbee</Term> lets many small devices talk to each other over short radio links, passing messages along until they reach the hub. It was designed for devices that send little data and must run for years on a battery.

The <Term id="coordinator">coordinator</Term> and every <Term id="zigbee-router">router</Term> must stay powered, because they relay for others. An <Term id="end-device">end device</Term> can sleep for minutes at a time, which is why a door sensor's coin cell lasts years.

## Mesh networking and self-healing

In a mesh, a message hops from router to router until it reaches its destination. If a router is unplugged, the network finds another route. This is called **self-healing**. End devices whose parent disappears re-join another router in range.

<Callout kind="remember" title="What if the coordinator fails?">
Routers keep passing messages along the routes they already have, so the mesh keeps working. What stops is new devices joining, because only the coordinator (trust centre) can let them in. In most homes the hub is also the gateway to the internet, so the app loses contact until the hub returns.
</Callout>

## Zigbee, Matter and the CSA

In 2021 the Zigbee Alliance renamed itself the <Term id="csa">Connectivity Standards Alliance (CSA)</Term>. The CSA also created <Term id="matter">Matter</Term>, a standard that lets smart-home devices from different makers work together.

Matter runs over **Wi-Fi, Thread and Ethernet**, not over Zigbee. Existing Zigbee devices join a Matter home through a **bridge**, usually the Zigbee hub itself.

<Callout kind="example" title="Smart lights in an Indian flat">
A family fits Zigbee bulbs in every room and a few battery door sensors. The bulbs are routers, so a sensor on the far balcony reaches the hub by hopping through the hall and living-room bulbs. If someone switches off the hall bulb at the wall, messages find a way round through the kitchen bulb instead.
</Callout>

## Advantages and limitations

**Advantages**

- Very low power: end devices last years on a coin cell.
- Mesh extends range through the building and heals itself.
- Large networks of cheap devices, with AES-128 security.

**Limitations**

- Low data rate: fine for commands and readings, not for audio or video.
- Needs a hub or gateway to reach phones and the internet.
- Shares the crowded 2.4 GHz band with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.
- Routers must stay powered; switching them off at the wall weakens the mesh.
